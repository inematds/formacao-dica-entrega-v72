Lesson 1 · 16 min

The first assistant: 3 criteria and what not to choose

The first assistant decides whether the owner trusts the second. Its job is not to solve the clinic's biggest problem: it is to prove, in a small, visible piece, that applied AI works in that operation. That is why the choice comes before any tool and ends in a one-page sheet, with what it does not do and how to shut it down.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Run the candidates from the map through the 3 criteria
  2. 2Recognize what should never be first
  3. 3Check the WhatsApp rules in 2026
  4. 4Write the sheet with the metric and the shutdown plan

Goal: choose the first assistant using the 3 criteria, write down what it does not do, and agree on the 30-day metric and the shutdown plan.

01 · From the map to the first assistant

Three filters in order, no skipping, and one channel check. Click each step.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

A filter that fails drops the candidate. Don't negotiate: the first case isn't the most exciting one, it's the one that gives a visible, safe win.

02 · What is never the first one

First projects tend to die in the same places. A consultant's rule of thumb.

1. Big system

Connecting the assistant to the clinic's management system depends on third parties and takes weeks.

Any failure on their side becomes your fault, before the owner sees any gain.

2. Touches money

Invoices, automatic discounts, and collecting overdue payments come later.

A wrong amount costs the trust that the first assistant was supposed to build.

3. Does everything

"I want a complete assistant" is the most common request and the most dangerous.

An open scope can't succeed and can't be measured.

4. No owner

The excited employee asks for it, the owner doesn't follow along.

If the owner doesn't feel the gain day to day, the project doesn't make it past the first month.

The rule of 3 no'sNo sponsorship from the owner, no start. No organized data (FAQ list, schedule), no start. No reversible mistake, no start.

03 · WhatsApp rules in 2026

Checked on the official pages on 10/19/2026. Platform rules change: check the date before each project.

1. Official channel

Use the WhatsApp Business app or Meta's official platform, through a recognized provider. Connectors that imitate the app are not an official channel and put the clinic's number at risk of being blocked.

Meta's policy allows automation within the 24-hour window as long as there is a quick, clear path to a person: transfer within the chat, phone, email, or an in-person visit.

2. Since 01/15/2026

The official platform's terms ban general-purpose AI chatbots as the main function, the "talk about anything" kind.

An assistant that answers questions, books appointments, confirms visits, and serves the customers of your own business is still allowed. Meta decides what counts as "main."

3. Billing

Since 07/01/2025, Meta charges per delivered message (before, it was per conversation), with a rate by category (marketing, utility, authentication and service) and by country.

Since 10/01/2026, each number gets 1,000 free service messages per month; from the 1,001st on, Meta charges, and without a payment method on file it stops delivering. The course gives no prices: open the official table on the day.

4. 24 h and consent

After the patient writes, the clinic can reply freely for 24 hours. Outside that window, only messages from a template approved by Meta can go out.

Reminders and campaigns only for people who agreed to receive them, with an easy way to opt out. The patient's number and conversation are personal data (LGPD, Brazil's data protection law): say what you use it for and how long you keep it.

The reminder has more points, but it doesn't need AI:
it goes out through the calendar, separately (the DICA 1 map already said so).
Collections: fails on low risk (it touches money).
Chosen for AI: questions and booking request.
1. DOES: new message on the front desk's WhatsApp →
   answer from the list of 15 questions or booking
   request noted for the front desk to confirm.
2. DOES NOT: package prices, rescheduling on its own,
   complaints, health questions.
4. PERSON: "talk to the front desk" at any time;
   outside hours, it says when the front desk is back.
6. METRIC: unanswered messages at the end of the day.
   Before: counted for 1 week by the front desk.
8. SHUTDOWN: the front desk goes back to answering from
   the phone the same day; the owner tells the team.

05 · Practice

Use Clínica Vila Serena (fictional) or the small business you serve, with the owner's permission.

1 · Matrix of candidates

20 min

List 5 candidates for the first assistant (from the DICA 1 map or from the pains the owner mentions) and fill in the matrix with the three scores. Run the highest-scoring one through the 3 nos rule.

Done when: the 5 candidates have all three scores, the points, and the columns "scope in one sentence?" and "needs AI?", and the chosen one has written down why it passes the 3 nos; the one that touches money, if there is one, is marked "later".

2 · One-page sheet

30 min

Fill in the first assistant sheet for the chosen candidate. Check the channel against the WhatsApp rules from this lesson.

Done when: the sheet fits on one page, all 8 fields are filled in, "does not do" has at least 3 items, the metric has the before number and where it comes from, and the shutdown plan says who does it and in how long.

07 · Summary

Three filtersHigh pain, low risk, clear scope, in that order. If one fails, drop the candidate.
Never firstBig system, money, "does everything," and a project without the owner.
Channel up to dateOfficial, with a path to a person, no general chatbot, with per-message charges checked against the date.
SheetOne page with what it doesn't do, the metric with the "before" number, and the shutdown plan.
