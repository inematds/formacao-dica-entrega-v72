Lesson 4 · 18 min

Build without becoming a factory: open kit, no-code, or nothing

Building got easy; what the owner is buying is knowing that what was built works. That's why this lesson doesn't teach a tool: it teaches how to decide. Sometimes nothing needs to be built, sometimes an open kit does the job, and sometimes a custom flow is worth it. In every case, 15 cases tested before you turn it on.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1First ask whether you can skip building
  2. 2See if an INEMA open kit already does the job
  3. 3The minimum for a custom flow that doesn't lock you in
  4. 4Test 15 cases before turning it on

Goal: decide between an open kit, a no-code flow, or nothing for the chosen assistant, and test 15 cases before turning it on.

02 · The three paths in practice

What to look at in each one before you choose.

1. Open kit

Rule-based conversation, scheduling, a list of common questions, a human queue, emergency words that call a person, campaigns only for people who gave consent, and export of patient data (LGPD, Brazil's data protection law).

Heads up: the kit connects WhatsApp through a connector that is NOT the official channel (it imitates the app). Don't use that part with customers: test through the website chat or the simulated outbox, and connecting the clinic's WhatsApp through the official platform is separate work.

2. No-code

A dedicated number for the assistant, never the owner's personal phone. A log of every run (what came in, what went out, playbook version). A copy of the flow kept outside the tool.

Access only to what the assistant needs. And the playbook stays in the clinic's document, not just inside the flow.

3. Costs

Tools, WhatsApp messages, and the model subscription stay on the clinic's account, paid by the clinic directly to the vendor. That way, when a price changes, nothing is hidden inside your service.

Don't memorize prices: open each vendor's official table on the day and note the link in the decision. How much to charge for your service is a topic for Selling with AI 4.

05 · Practice

For the assistant on the lesson 1 worksheet, with the playbook from lesson 2.

1 · Written decision

30 min

Fill in the build decision. Open the guide for the open kit closest to the niche and note what it covers and what is missing.

Done when: the three questions each have an answer with a reason, the choice is in one sentence, there is a "what stays out" line, and each clinic account has an account holder and a link to the price table with the date checked, with no copied price.

2 · 15-case test set

45 min

Write the 15 cases with the expected answer before you run anything. Run them on the kit, on the flow, or in a hand test with the playbook, and note the result of each one.

Done when: there are 15 cases with the expected answer written first, at least 2 that call a person and 1 forbidden request, the result of every case is noted, and the last round did not break any limit.
