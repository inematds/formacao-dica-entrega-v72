Lesson 7 · 17 min

Maintain and standardize: maintenance, next area and niche library

The assistant running is not the end. The model changes, the WhatsApp rule changes, the clinic changes. This lesson shows what stays after the handoff: a written maintenance scope, a 12-month map for the clinic, a library that lets the next client in the same niche start from the middle, and a checklist so nothing gets forgotten.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Customize the front, standardize the back
  2. 2Write down what maintenance includes and doesn't include
  3. 3Choose the next area at the right time
  4. 4Run the checklist before every handoff

Goal: write the maintenance scope without a price, build the clinic's 12-month map and run the checklist before handing off.

02 · The maintenance scope

Write down what is in and what is out. The price and how to sell it are covered in Selling with AI 4 and 5.

1. Includes

Check-in on the agreed cadence, versioned playbook adjustments, updates to the manual and the list of open questions, a monthly report, and support within an agreed timeframe.

And every quarter, check whether any rule has changed for WhatsApp, the vendor, or LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) that affects the assistant.

2. Does not include

A new assistant, a new area, integration with another system, social media content, a website.

A request outside the scope becomes a conversation about a new project, not a favor.

3. Easy exit

The clinic can leave with the agreed notice, with no penalty, taking the playbook, manual, and records with them, because those belong to the clinic.

A client who stays because they choose to trusts you more than a client held by a contract.

03 · Next area and library

One area at a time; the next client in the niche starts from the middle.

1. When

Only after the first one is stable and the gain has been shown against the baseline.

At the check-in, take 10 minutes: "If I could help unblock one thing in the next two months, what hurts the most today?" There may be no next area: that is fine.

2. Library

Save it as soon as you do it, with a version, and remove names, phone numbers, and amounts before saving.

Every quarter, retire what nobody used and promote to standard what keeps repeating.

3. "We're different"

Almost always it is the word that changes, not the flow: "patient record" instead of "contact". Change the field name and keep the structure.

If the owner insists, ask for a concrete example before changing the method.

06 · Practice

For Vila Serena (fictional) or the business you serve.

1 · Scope and 12-month map

40 min

Write the maintenance scope and the clinic's 12-month map from what you did in lessons 1 to 6.

Done when: the scope has at least 5 items under "includes", 4 under "does not include", an exit with no penalty, and no price in reais; the map covers all 12 months, only opens the next area after the month the gain is shown, and includes the review of WhatsApp rules and LGPD.

2 · Checklist and first library folder

30 min

Run the pre-delivery checklist on the clinic's assistant and store in the library, in a niche folder, the playbook and the "important" table without client data.

Done when: every checklist item has yes, no, or not applicable with evidence, each "no" has an action and a date, and the niche folder exists with a playbook and a table without any client name, phone, or price.
