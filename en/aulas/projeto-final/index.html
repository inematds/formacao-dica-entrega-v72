Rubric (0 to 4 per criterion)
RUBRIC · DICA DOSSIER · score from 0 to 4 on each criterion
Done when: all criteria at 3 or higher.

1. PAIN WITH A NUMBER
   0 no number · 2 number with no way to measure it · 4 baseline with date, who measured, and how

2. CLOSED SCOPE
   0 "complete assistant" · 2 does is described, does-not is vague · 4 one area, "does not" with 3+ items

3. SAFETY
   0 no phases · 2 phases without a written rule · 4 high risk in phase 1, path to a person
     tested, official channel, personal data with purpose and deadline

4. THE CLINIC USES IT WITHOUT YOU
   0 only the consultant operates it · 2 champion without a manual · 4 champion approves and adjusts on her own,
     manual at the front desk, questions log in use

5. FOLLOW-UP
   0 no report · 2 reports without a baseline · 4 four reports against the
     baseline, maintenance scope, and next step agreed (including stopping)

    07 · The course in four sentences
    Choose smallHigh pain, low risk, clear scope, an official channel, and a shutdown plan.
    Turn on in a ladderA versioned playbook, 15 tested cases, and 3 phases with a way back.

    Follow up with numbersA 20-minute check-in, 5 indicators against the baseline, and a cadence that tapers off.
    08 · What changed compared to the Customer Map (October 2026 update)
    WhatsAppOfficial channel with a path to a person; general-purpose chatbot banned on the official platform since 01/15/2026; charging per delivered message since 07/01/2025, with 1,000 free service messages per number per month since 10/01/2026. Checked on 10/19/2026.
    No prices, no statisticsAmounts in reais and percentages without a source were removed. The phase criterion became a rule agreed with the owner; pricing is in Selling with AI.
    Open kitThe "ready-made packages" became diagnosis + INEMA open kit + follow-up, with 15 cases tested before going live.
    LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) and the projectRecordings, screenshots, and patient data only with a stated purpose, a time limit, and authorization. The final project uses a fictional company; a real case is optional.
